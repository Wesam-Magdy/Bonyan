# BONYAN — Brand Strategy One-Pager
**Direction: A — The Monolith | Locked: 17 May 2026**

---

## Positioning Statement
Bonyan is Egypt's only vertically integrated stone-to-structure contractor — the single partner that owns the quarry, runs the workshop, installs the finish, and maintains the building. For government bodies, defence clients, and premium developers who cannot afford failure, Bonyan delivers institutional-grade quality with unbroken chain of custody from Aswan granite to finished surface.

## Brand Promise
"One accountable partner. From the quarry to the handover key."

## The Single Most Important Feeling
**Unshakeable trust.** When a client sees the Bonyan mark, they should feel the same confidence they feel handing a project to the Egyptian Armed Forces Engineering Authority — it will be done right, on time, to specification, without excuses.

## Personality Compass
| Trait | We Are | We Are Not |
|---|---|---|
| Confident | Measured, assured | Arrogant, loud |
| Premium | Refined, quality-led | Expensive for its own sake |
| Institutional | Credible, government-grade | Bureaucratic, cold |
| Egyptian | Proud of origin, Aswan roots | Folksy or tourist-facing |

## Design Decision Anchor
Every visual decision must pass this test:
> "Would this mark sit comfortably on a Presidential Palace specification document?"
If yes — proceed. If not — revise.

## What the Logo Must Communicate
1. Authority (not a startup, not informal)
2. Precision (stone is unforgiving — so is the mark)
3. Heritage (gold = legacy, not decoration)
4. Egyptian pride (the name بنيان means Foundation — the mark should feel foundational)

## Colour Lock
- Primary: Deep Navy `#0D1B2A`
- Accent: Heritage Gold `#C8960C`
- Background (light): Parchment `#F8F4EE`
- Supporting: Slate `#4A5568`

## Typography Lock
- Display/Logo: Serif (refined, weight-bearing)
- Body/UI: Clean sans-serif (neutral, legible)
- Never: Script, decorative, or condensed display fonts
