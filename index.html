About Bonyan

BONYAN Contracting & Supplies, operating under Bonyan Group, is a leading provider of comprehensive solutions in construction, granite quarrying, supply and application services, as well as operations and maintenance (O&M) for hospitals and buildings.

We pride ourselves on delivering high-quality services with a focus on safety, innovation, and client satisfaction. With a proven track record across major infrastructure and commercial projects in Egypt, Bonyan has established itself as a trusted partner to contractors, consultants, and owners alike.

Stone & Granite

From Quarry to Installation —
Total Stone Capability

Direct ownership of quarrying operations in two strategic locations gives Bonyan unmatched control over quality, supply, and cost.

Aswan

Upper Egypt — Direct Quarry Access

Aswan granite is among Egypt's finest — renowned for its durability, deep colour, and consistent grain. Our Aswan operations supply premium stone for flooring, cladding, and structural applications across Egypt's landmark projects.

Marsa Alam

Red Sea Region — Direct Quarry Access

Our Red Sea quarry operations yield distinctive stone varieties suited to both exterior and interior applications. Strategic location enables efficient supply to projects across the New Administrative Capital and Red Sea development corridor.

Quality & HSE

Standards That Never
Compromise

Every project is governed by our Quality Management System, Project Quality Plan, and HSE framework — from first stone to final handover.

Quality Management System

End-to-end QMS governing all project activities — from procurement through installation and handover.

Project Quality Plan

Project-specific quality planning and compliance documentation prepared and approved before works commence.

HSE Plan

Health, Safety & Environment plan enforced on all sites — protecting our workforce, clients, and surrounding communities.

Project Specification

Strict adherence to technical specifications per project — zero deviation from approved shop drawings and method statements.

Project Team Structure

Project Manager

Project planning, scheduling, client coordination, handover, and approval of work procedures.

Site Engineer

Component installation to schedule, team leadership, and preparation of inspection reports.

Manufacturing Engineer

Supervising manufacturing, pre-supply inspection & testing, and coordinating delivery deadlines.

Technical Office

Preparation and approval of shop drawings, material submittals, and method statements.

Quality Control

Pre-supply component inspection and testing, powder coating verification, and material compliance.
